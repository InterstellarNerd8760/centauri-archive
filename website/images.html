    THE CURATION STANDARD

    Evidence, not decoration.

    Scientific context

    Every image should have a clear claim, scale, relationship or observation and disclose when it is schematic, composite, enhanced or not to scale.

    Representative selection

    Show microorganisms, ecosystems and different human societies—not just famous monuments, astronauts and large mammals.

    Preservation proof

    Each selected image needs legal redistribution rights, source provenance, a tested readout encoding and a smaller fallback where appropriate.
