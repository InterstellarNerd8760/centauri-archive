02 / INTERPRETATION

How would anyone read it?

We cannot assume the reader knows English, computers, ZIP files or the concept of a byte. The goal is a physical-to-digital learning sequence — currently a research plan.

  1. Recognize structure

    Repeated marks, patterns, orientation and counting.

  2. Establish shared references

    Mathematical relationships, units and diagrams grounded in physics.

  3. Decode stored information

    Framing, ordered symbols, test images and error recovery.

  4. Understand the record

    The Solar System, chemistry, biology, and then human civilization.

Digital package

Build, inspect and verify original content and a manifest with file lengths and SHA-256 checksums. These detect corruption, not repair it.
