A record
of Earth.

An intelligible record of a living world.

Centauri Archive is an independent project to preserve knowledge of Earth, its biosphere, and human civilization in a compact record intended for the extraordinary distances and timescales of interstellar travel.

A growing scientific and cultural collection, alongside experiments in how a future reader might reconstruct its meaning.

THE PROJECT

Preserve knowledge. Explain how to read it.

Centauri Archive complements the idea behind Voyager's Golden Record with a progressive scientific introduction and independently recoverable records.

01

The knowledge collection

Foundational scientific and cultural articles, connected to real photographs and diagrams. Source-checked drafts await independent review.

02

The decoding method

Start with observable marks, then learn mathematics, image reconstruction and a visual vocabulary. Explore the recipient's discovery sequence.

03

The physical artifact

Explore candidate storage media, error recovery and capacity. No physical carrier has been fabricated or qualified.

04

The mission proposal

Centauri Core is the compact baseline; Extended is an optional larger archive. The proposal requests feasibility guidance, not an approved payload.

03 / ENGINEERING

Designed to be tested.

Reproducible data, transparent assumptions, and a path from experiments to potential mission integration.
