THE PROJECT / IN BRIEF

What is Centauri Archive?

An open effort to make a compact, intelligible record of Earth for long-duration spaceflight. The work has three distinct parts: deciding what to preserve, finding a way to explain it, and designing a medium that can survive.

WORKING PROTOTYPE

A reproducible digital collection

Original introductory records, a versioned manifest, SHA-256 integrity checks and automated tests. Browse the collection or inspect the source and build tools.

Explore the collection ↗

ACTIVE RESEARCH

A physical decoding primer

Early visual examples of counting and image reconstruction. We have not demonstrated that an unfamiliar reader can decode the information independently.

NOT YET BUILT

A spacecraft-ready artifact

A passive engraved carrier is one candidate. Material, mass, size, mounting, durability and mission compatibility remain undetermined.

Why a new archive?

Voyager's Golden Record is an enduring cultural portrait of Earth. Centauri Archive explores a different, complementary direction: a structured introduction to our scientific understanding, supported by diagrams and a carefully tested decoding method. It is not a claim to supersede Voyager or represent all humanity.

02 / INTERPRETATION

How would anyone read it?

We cannot assume the reader knows English, computers, ZIP files or the concept of a byte. The goal is a physical-to-digital learning sequence — currently a research plan.

  1. Recognize structure

    Repeated marks, patterns, orientation and counting.

  2. Establish shared references

    Mathematical relationships, units and diagrams grounded in physics.

  3. Decode stored information

    Framing, ordered symbols, test images and error recovery.

  4. Understand the record

    The Solar System, chemistry, biology, and then human civilization.

03 / ENGINEERING

Designed to be tested.

Reproducible data, transparent assumptions, and a path from experiments to potential mission integration.

WORKING TODAY

Digital package

Build, inspect and verify original content and a manifest with file lengths and SHA-256 checksums. These detect corruption, not repair it.

RESEARCH

Physical preservation

A passive decoding-primer concept. No fabricated carrier, lifetime estimate or tested extraterrestrial decoding yet.

UNCONFIRMED

Mission fit

Mass, dimensions, environment, telemetry and any available payload allocation need direct confirmation from the mission team.
