Centauri Core
for Fermi Explorer.

A proposal to investigate carrying a compact record of Earth, life and human knowledge. It would pair a directly observable decoding primer with independently recoverable scientific and cultural records.

Centauri Archive has no approved allocation, integration agreement or verified spacecraft interface. We seek a feasibility discussion and a technical path to evaluate a passive demonstrator.

Purpose and mission context

Voyager paired a record of sounds and images with engraved instructions for playback. Pioneer demonstrated a message cut directly onto a surface, and Arecibo used an ordered bit sequence to convey an image. Centauri Core builds on these precedents with a compact collection of linked scientific ideas, photographic records, and open reconstruction and recovery instructions. A future reader’s ability to find the artifact or interpret its meaning remains uncertain.

NASA Voyager coverPioneer authors’ paperNAIC Arecibo paper

Fermi Explorer’s FAQ gives a public objective of approaching Alpha Centauri within 80,000 years and carrying at least 1 kg of payload in a 10 × 10 × 10 cm envelope. The FAQ and September 1 announcement state an intent to include a Golden Record copy. The July 2026 PSI report studies solar-electric perihelion pumping against a different initial arrival bound and discloses that it did not undergo comprehensive human peer review. Each source describes public aims or architecture studies. Centauri Archive would need contributor-specific constraints from the mission’s integration team.

Archive editions

Centauri Core

≈100 MB or less

The baseline proposed for initial consideration. It combines a physical mathematics and science primer, curated photographs and diagrams, an encyclopedia of essentials, a visual vocabulary, cultural and historical records, selected messages, and encoding specifications and test vectors.

Centauri Extended

≈64 GB or more

An optional, separately recoverable expansion with its own Core copy. It would investigate full or nearly full English Wikipedia article text with attribution, additional scientific and cultural records, and multilingual material and media where practical.

These decimal content targets are provisional. Error-correction symbols and redundant copies add stored bytes. Substrate, shielding and mounts affect mass and volume. Core carries its own primer, framing and specifications and can be recovered independently of Extended.

A progressive archive

Orientation and readout witnesses lead to mathematics, image reconstruction, vocabulary and Essential knowledge. A curated expansion can contribute to Core where measured budgets permit. Extended may add a broader collection and an optional Wikipedia-derived module.

Software has reconstructed the Earth raster from complementary damaged copies and recovered individual records without a surviving map. A passive demonstrator would test whether optical sampling, physical access and interpretation can support those results.

Carrier, decoding and preservation

An exterior primer would point to finer passive markings and a dense archive. Candidate positions range from an inspectable plate to a protected internal stack, with another copy elsewhere if placement permits. Optional electronic storage would depend on granted interfaces. The mission FAQ expects the spacecraft to lose power after it leaves the Solar System. Mission FAQ, spacecraft design

The teaching sequence starts with distinguishable states and orientation, then adds quantity, measurement, and the connection between ordered symbols and a raster. Each recoverable block would protect its metadata with its payload. A record digest checks the result of correction. The scheme’s repair limits and behavior under damage must be measured and documented.

Open design questions include mount and exposure, thermal and radiation environments, impact damage, optical readout, retention, and what an unfamiliar observer can infer from the physical primer.

From prototypes to a physical demonstrator

Today the project maintains a verified digital development package, sourced photographs, introductory encyclopedia drafts, and bench experiments in decoding and error recovery. No physical carrier or spacecraft installation has been built or qualified.

  1. Feasibility screening. Confirm the prospective contribution pathway and the applicable interface inputs.
  2. Encoding and recovery. Demonstrate deterministic photographic encoding, independent reconstruction and documented outcomes under controlled damage.
  3. Content and interpretation. Review sources, rights and scientific claims. Use independent trials to measure what participants infer from the primer.
  4. Physical demonstrator. Fabricate coupon specimens, document instrument and readout requirements, and investigate materials and assembly options.
  5. Integration and qualification. Review mount, mass, environment, schedule and applicable qualification with the spacecraft team.

Request to the mission team

Would Fermi Explorer consider a passive archive demonstrator under a prospective scientific or cultural payload pathway? If so, we request a technical point of contact, the applicable interface and verification documents, and guidance on the relevant design and solicitation deadlines.

Centauri Archive could contribute content curation, encoding software and a bounded concept study for review. Physical fabrication, materials selection, qualification, funding and integration responsibilities would need to be agreed with the mission team and qualified engineering or manufacturing collaborators. This proposal does not commit the project to delivering flight hardware independently.
