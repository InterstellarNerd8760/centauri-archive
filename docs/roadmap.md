## v0.5 — engineering evidence
- [ ] Implement, benchmark and test error correction and interleaving.

- [ ] Quantify mass, geometry and tradeoffs for candidate carriers.
- [ ] Test hardware only against verified mission requirements.

## Submission gate
- [ ] Identify mission technical contact and confirm any cultural payload slot.
- [ ] Obtain actual spacecraft mechanical/environmental/interface constraints.
- [ ] Submit a technical proposal with measurements and unambiguous claims.
- [ ] Do not publicly claim mission affiliation without written agreement.

**Long-term:** Ark Edition is a separate research program, not a promise of
near-term spacecraft compatibility.
