# Photographic acquisitions — Batch 001

**Status:** Four real photographs are preserved as JPEG derivatives in the verified development ZIP. Their source masters remain accessible at the listed archive source pages; the original master scans are **not** yet stored in this repository. This set is an initial editorial acquisition, not an approved Fermi Explorer payload or a claim of 100 completed photographs.

## Why actual photography?

The artistic mission is closer to Voyager's Golden Record: a carefully selected, truthful and memorable portrait of the Solar System, the living Earth and human civilization. Synthetic illustrations can clarify concepts in the separate scientific reader, but they must not be passed off as documentary images. The 100-photograph plan explicitly counts only real optical, space-based or scientific photographic observations.

### Exact provenance of the copies

The archived JPEG bytes were copied **without additional changes** from WebKit/Speedometer's [photographic test assets](https://github.com/WebKit/Speedometer/tree/main/suites-experimental/pdf-viewer/assets). Its documented [photo provenance](https://github.com/WebKit/Speedometer/blob/main/suites-experimental/pdf-viewer/assets/README.md) says the files were downloaded from Commons and processed once with ImageMagick (auto-orient; resized to 1400×1400, 1200×1200 or 1120×1400 bounding boxes; JPEG quality 82; chroma subsampling 4:2:0; metadata stripped). This is appropriate for a website proof-of-concept but **not** a substitute for preserving original full-resolution master imagery.

Because the images are public-domain U.S. federal works, crediting NASA, the National Park Service, and named photographers is a documentation and scientific provenance requirement, even where domestic copyright law does not impose attribution. National insignia and use implying NASA/NPS mission endorsement remain restricted. Public-domain status outside the U.S. may vary.

No image in this batch was AI-generated, drawn as SVG, or hotlinked for display. The files reside in the archive and are included in the reproducible ZIP. New photographs still need scientific caption review and final editorial selection.

## What is not done

- The full 100-image selection is not complete. Exactly four real photographs are in the collection; 96 slots are open.
- The three original SVG science diagrams are *not* counted as these photographs. They remain useful explanatory records.
- The originals / archival master scans are not stored locally; they must be preserved in a larger master collection before long-term preservation claims.
- No physical encoding has been selected. JPEG decoding depends on Earth-designed conventions. The physical primer must teach a simpler raw format first.
- The set is heavily weighted toward spaceflight and U.S. federal imagery. The next batches must feature microorganisms, ecosystems, animals, plants, architecture, family life, human culture, and geographic diversity.

## Quality acceptance before we can call a photograph final

1. An authentic observation with trustworthy chain of custody; identify composites and processing.
2. Full-resolution original or losslessly preserved master, separate from a small web derivative.
3. A concise factual caption including what, where, how and when; careful about assumptions.
4. Source origin, photographer, rights and license notes, derivative history and image checksum.
5. Authentic geographical, biological and cultural representation across the complete selection.
6. The final encoded version recoverable through a physically demonstrated format (still unproven).

The next photo sourcing priorities should be living nature and human experience, not additional space mission promotional images.
