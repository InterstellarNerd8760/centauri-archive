Life / A leaf as a boundary

Hold a leaf toward the light. Trace a large vein from its stalk, then follow a
smaller branch toward the edge. Turn the leaf over and compare the same route.

The outline records a surface. The veins record paths through it. A pressed
leaf can keep both shapes while losing color, flexibility and the exchange of
water and gas that once took place. Preserving a shape preserves only part
of the living thing.
