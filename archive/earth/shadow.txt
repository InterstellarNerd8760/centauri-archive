Earth / A record made with a stick

Stand a straight stick upright on level ground in sunlight. Mark the tip of its
shadow. Return later and mark the tip again, leaving the stick in place. The
marks are a record of a changing direction measured against one patch of Earth.

A drawing of the marks alone loses information. A second observer needs the
height of the stick, the local slope, the place and the times. A record of a
planet begins as much with those choices as with the shape on the ground.
